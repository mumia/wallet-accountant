# Planning

What a Household expects to earn and spend, and how its Accounts' balances will develop.

## Language

**Budget**:
A planned amount for one Income or Expense Category over a month or a year, compared against what actually happened. Activity in a sub-Category also counts toward the Budgets of every Category above it.
_Avoid_: Envelope, limit, goal, income plan

**Scheduled transaction**:
A template for money expected on known dates, such as a monthly salary or yearly insurance, with an Account, a Category, an expected amount and a recurrence. Its occurrences feed Forecasts and are Matched to the real transactions when they arrive.
_Avoid_: Recurring payment, standing order, forecast entry

**Forecast**:
The projected future balance of an Account, built from its current balance and its upcoming Scheduled transactions.
_Avoid_: Projection, prediction, cash-flow plan
